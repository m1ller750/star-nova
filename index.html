Install in a minute

  1. Download StarNova-Setup-1.0.0.exe with the button above.
  2. Run it. Setup installs for your user only, so no administrator rights are needed. If Windows SmartScreen says "Windows protected your PC", click More info → Run anyway: the installer is not code-signed yet.
  3. Play from the Start menu or the desktop shortcut. Headphones recommended: every line of the story is voiced.

Remove it any time from Windows Settings → Apps → Star Nova. Your saved progress stays.

System requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • A DirectX 12 graphics card (tested on an NVIDIA GeForce GTX 1650)
  • 8 GB RAM, 300 MB of disk space
  • Keyboard and mouse

Built with Unity 6 and the Universal Render Pipeline: soft shadows, ambient occlusion, HDR bloom, photo-scanned materials and real NASA imagery of the planets.

The story

In the year 2300 the Klin'Tar Empire began conquering every inhabited world of the Milky Way. In 2500 it reached Earth: it drained Mars, seized Mercury and Venus, and turned its dreadnoughts toward the home world. Humanity answered with a living weapon, Nova. Five star systems stand between Nova and the master of the Klin'Tar, and the last one holds a secret that reads exactly like Nova.

Five real star systems

Every star, planet and black hole comes from NASA data: the NASA Exoplanet Archive, the NASA Planetary Fact Sheet and NASA Chandra. Only the comets are invented.

LEVEL 1

Solar System

Dog-fight above Earth, then land on Mercury, Venus and Mars to liberate them. Earth, the Moon, Mercury, Mars and the gas giants wear real NASA imagery: Blue Marble, MESSENGER, Clementine and Viking.

LEVEL 2

Kepler-90

An eight-planet system 2,545 light-years away. Board the Klin'Tar colonizer ring, free the hostages before a five-minute fusion charge goes off, and visit the Keplerian home world.

LEVEL 3

TRAPPIST-1

Seven Earth-sized worlds around an ultracool dwarf. Storm three fortresses, fly on foot in open space, and stop a public execution in the bazaar city of TRAPPIST-1 e.

LEVEL 4

Cygnus X-1

The first black hole ever confirmed: 21 solar masses feeding on the blue supergiant HDE 226868. It's a trap. Eject before the missile hits and fight the void creatures in your suit.

LEVEL 5

HR 8799

Four directly imaged super-Jupiters. Hurl comets into dreadnoughts, break the Shadow Guard and face the master of the Klin'Tar on the throne world.

On the ground

Controls

Starship

Mouse aim · W/S throttle · A/D roll · Shift boost · left mouse cannons · right mouse rockets · Q shield · F laser · X eject · E land · J light-speed jump

On foot and flying

WASD move · Space jump (again in the air to fly) · C crouch · Shift sprint · 1–5 weapons · mouse attack / aim · F melee and silent takedown · R missiles · Q eye lasers · G smoke · E interact · V first / third person · Esc pause

Every level is open from the start: choose SELECT LEVEL on the title screen or in the pause menu.
